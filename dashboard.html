Terms and Conditions

Purpose

Sick Sense is a community-based health monitoring system designed to help monitor health conditions, organize records, and support early health awareness.

Information Collected

We may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Height / Weight / BMI
  • Address
  • Contact Number
  • Symptoms or health-related entries
  • Login credentials

User Responsibilities

You agree to:

  • Provide accurate information
  • Keep account credentials secure
  • Use the system lawfully
  • Avoid unauthorized access

Data Privacy

Your health data is encrypted and stored securely. We do not share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

Limitations

Sick Sense is not a replacement for professional medical advice. Always consult with qualified healthcare providers for medical decisions.
